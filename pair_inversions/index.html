What counts as an inversion?

A pair of positions i < j with ai > aj. For 2 4 1 3, the pairs (2, 1), (4, 1), (4, 3) give X = 3. All pairs count, including nonadjacent ones.

Why the tilted strips?

Each row has the same relative ordering in its tail. Starting with k adds k − 1 inversions, so X = tail inversions + k − 1. Counts rise by one across a row. Equal colors trace diagonal fragments; the pattern resets as the tail ordering changes.
